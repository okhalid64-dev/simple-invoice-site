Privacy Policy

Simple Invoice & Estimate — Next Layer Technologies
Last updated: August 29, 2026

The short version

Simple Invoice & Estimate stores all of your data on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. The app works entirely offline and does not require an account.

Information stored on your device

The business details, logo, client information, estimates, and invoices you enter are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to any third party by the app. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Information we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking.

Sharing documents

When you share a PDF (for example by email or a messaging app), the document is handed to the app you choose. That app's own privacy policy applies to anything you send through it.

Purchases

Optional subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google's handling of billing information is described in the Google Play Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is a general-audience business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date.
